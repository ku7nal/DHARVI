# 01 — Low-poly building archetype generator

**What to build:** Replace the plain building volume presentation with lightweight procedural low-poly building archetypes that visibly read as houses or stylized structures, including walls, roofs, and simple proportions.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A detected building can render as a recognizable low-poly house or building rather than a plain blob.
- [ ] At least two roof/building variations are supported while reusing lightweight geometry and materials.
- [ ] Buildings remain grounded, height-scaled, layer-toggleable, and compatible with wireframe mode.
